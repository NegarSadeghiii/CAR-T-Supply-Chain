Core Idea

Manufacturing time $T_{MF}$ is uncertain. Once cells are ready, we can choose how fast to ship them back. That is the only recourse action available — and it is enough.

Stage 1 — Before $T_{MF}$ is Known

  • Open facility $z_m$
  • Assign patient to route $x_i$
    (outbound hub $j^{out}$, facility $m$, standard return hub $j^{ret}$)
  • These decisions are fixed once executed

Stage 2 — After $T_{MF}$ is Revealed

  • Observe actual $\tilde T_{MF,p}$
  • If patient would be late with standard return: expedite
  • Pay premium $\pi_p$, return time reduced by $\delta$ days
  • If on time with standard return: do nothing

No Penalty Terms — Ever

The objective contains only transport cost, facility cost, and expected expediting cost. There is no lateness penalty, no soft-deadline term, no violation cost. Deadlines are enforced at the plan-generation stage: only routes where $K_i + \delta \ge 0$ (deadline achievable even with worst-case expediting benefit) are kept.

The Two Stages in Detail

Stage 1: Plan Selection

Select one route per patient and decide which facilities to open. This is the same binary assignment problem as deterministic ColGen.

$$\text{For each plan } i:\quad \text{slack}\; K_i = D_p - T_{LS} - TT1_{j^{out}} - T_{QC} - TT3_{j^{ret}}$$

$K_i$ is how much manufacturing time is available before the patient is late using standard return. Plans where $K_i + \delta < 0$ are removed — even expedited return cannot save the deadline.

Stage 2: Expediting Decision

After $\tilde T_{MF,p}$ is revealed, the recourse rule is simple:

$\tilde T_{MF} \le K_i$

On time with standard return.
Action: do nothing.
Extra cost: $0$

$K_i < \tilde T_{MF} \le K_i + \delta$

Late with standard, on time with expedited.
Action: expedite return.
Extra cost: $\pi_p$

$\tilde T_{MF} > K_i + \delta$

Late even with expediting. This route was filtered out at Stage 1 — it cannot appear here.
Extra cost: $0$ (infeasible route removed)

Why Zone 3 Never Occurs

The plan-generation filter ensures $K_i + \delta \ge \bar T_{MF}$ at minimum (or uses a CCP-style quantile for a probabilistic guarantee). Routes where $T_{MF}$ could exceed $K_i + \delta$ with non-negligible probability are excluded. So the recourse is always binary: expedite or do nothing.

Full Formulation

Uncertainty Model

$$T_{MF} \sim \text{LogNormal}(\mu_L,\,\sigma_L^2),\quad \mu_L = \ln(\bar T_{MF}) - \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma_L^2,\quad \mathbb{E}[T_{MF}] = \bar T_{MF} = 7\text{ days}$$

Plan Feasibility Filter

$$K_i + \delta \;\ge\; F^{-1}(1-\varepsilon) \qquad \text{(deadline achievable with expediting at probability } 1-\varepsilon\text{)}$$

where $F^{-1}(1-\varepsilon)$ is the $(1-\varepsilon)$-quantile of $T_{MF}$. Routes that fail this are removed. This is a simple scalar check — no solver required.

Objective

$$\min_{x,z} \quad \underbrace{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} c_i\, x_i}_{\text{transport}} \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{m \in M} f_m\, z_m}_{\text{facility}} \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} x_i \cdot \pi_{p(i)} \cdot \Pr(T_{MF} > K_i)}_{\text{expected expediting cost}}$$

Constraints (identical to deterministic ColGen)

$$\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_p} x_i = 1 \quad \forall p \in P \tag{Assign}$$ $$\sum_{m \in M} z_m \le M_{\max} \tag{FacLimit}$$ $$x_i \le z_{m(i)} \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{I} \tag{FacLink}$$ $$\sum_{i:\,m(i)=m,\,t\in[s_i,s_i+\bar T_{MF})} x_i \le FCAP_m \quad \forall m,t \tag{Cap}$$ $$x_i \in \{0,1\},\quad z_m \in \{0,1\} \tag{Binary}$$

Modified Column Cost

$$\tilde c_i = c_i^{\text{transport}} + \pi_{p(i)} \cdot \Pr(T_{MF} > K_i) \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad \min \sum_i \tilde c_i\,x_i + \sum_m f_m z_m$$

The master IP structure is identical to deterministic ColGen. Only the column cost changes. No scenarios. No Benders. No second-stage solver.

Expected Expediting Cost — Closed Form

The expected recourse cost for plan $i$ is simply the expediting premium times the probability that expediting is needed:

$$\mathbb{E}[R_i] = \pi_{p(i)} \cdot \Pr(T_{MF} > K_i)$$

Each Term Explained

$\pi_{p(i)}$ — Expediting Premium

The extra cost to use fast-courier return instead of standard return. Depends on urgency group:

Group$\pi_p$
High$8,000
Medium$4,000
Low$1,500

$\Pr(T_{MF} > K_i)$ — Probability of Needing to Expedite

Probability that manufacturing overruns the slack. For $T_{MF} \sim \text{LogNormal}(\mu_L, \sigma_L^2)$:

$$\Pr(T_{MF} > K_i) = 1 - \Phi\!\left(\frac{\ln K_i - \mu_L}{\sigma_L}\right)$$

$\Phi$ = standard normal CDF. When $K_i = \bar T_{MF}$ (tight deadline): probability $\approx 0.5$. When $K_i \gg \bar T_{MF}$: probability $\to 0$.

Intuition

What the Formula Says

A plan with large slack $K_i$ (generous deadline) has low $\Pr(T_{MF}>K_i)$ — expediting is rarely needed, so its expected expediting cost is low.

A plan with tight slack (deadline barely fits) has high $\Pr(T_{MF}>K_i)$ — expediting is often needed, raising the effective column cost.

The optimizer therefore prefers routes with slack when the expediting premium is high relative to transport cost differences.

Step-by-Step Calculation

1
Compute $\mu_L = \ln(\bar T_{MF}) - \frac{1}{2}\sigma_L^2$. With $\bar T_{MF}=7$, $\sigma_L=0.2$: $\mu_L = 1.926$
2
For each plan $i$, compute slack $K_i = D_p - T_{LS} - TT1_{j^{out}} - T_{QC} - TT3_{j^{ret}}$
3
Filter: remove plans where $K_i + \delta < F^{-1}(1-\varepsilon)$ (infeasible even with expediting)
4
Compute $\Pr(T_{MF}>K_i) = 1 - \Phi\!\left(\frac{\ln K_i - \mu_L}{\sigma_L}\right)$ for each remaining plan
5
Set $\tilde c_i = c_i^{\rm transport} + \pi_{p(i)} \cdot \Pr(T_{MF}>K_i)$
6
Solve master IP with $\tilde c_i$ — same solver, same structure as deterministic ColGen

Comparison: Deterministic vs CCP vs Two-Stage Expediting

Deterministic ColGen

Obj: $\min\sum c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
Filter: $K_i \ge \bar T_{MF}$
Recourse: none
Under uncertainty: may be late with no response
Tuning: none

CCP Stochastic ColGen

Obj: $\min\sum c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
Filter: $K_i \ge F^{-1}(1-\alpha)$
Recourse: none
Under uncertainty: guaranteed $\Pr(\text{on time})\ge 1-\alpha$
Tuning: $\alpha$

Two-Stage + Expediting (this)

Obj: $\min\sum \tilde c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
$\tilde c_i = c_i + \pi_p \cdot \Pr(T_{MF}>K_i)$
Filter: $K_i + \delta \ge F^{-1}(1-\varepsilon)$
Recourse: expedite when $T_{MF}>K_i$
Under uncertainty: minimises expected total cost
Tuning: $\pi_p$, $\delta$, $\varepsilon$
