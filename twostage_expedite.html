What Recourse Actions Are Feasible in CAR-T?

Once Stage 1 decisions are executed (facility opened, route booked, cells shipped outbound), the question is: what can we do after we observe the actual manufacturing duration?

Reassign to Different Facility — NOT Feasible

  • Apheresis cells have already been shipped to facility m
  • Manufacturing has already started — cells are being engineered
  • CAR-T cells are patient-specific: one patient, one batch, one facility
  • You cannot move partially-manufactured cells to m′

This is a biological constraint, not a modelling choice.

Expedite Return Logistics — YES, Feasible

  • Manufacturing is finished — cells are ready to ship back
  • Standard courier: $TT3$ days at cost $c^{ret}$
  • Expedited courier: $TT3 - \delta$ days at cost $c^{ret} + \pi_p$
  • Decision made after $T_{MF}$ is observed
  • If expediting saves more lateness penalty than its cost, choose expedite

This is a genuine Stage 2 decision with a clear threshold rule.

The Two-Stage Structure

Stage 1 — Before TMF is known:
Open facility $z_m$, assign patient to route $x_i$ (outbound hub $j^{out}$, facility $m$, standard return hub $j^{ret}$)
Stage 2 — After TMF is revealed:
Observe actual manufacturing time. Decide: pay premium $\pi_p$ for faster return (saves $\delta$ days), or accept lateness penalty $w_p$ per day late.

Stage 2 Recourse Decision

Given the Stage 1 assignment $x_i = 1$ (plan $i$ selected for patient $p$), define:

$$K_i = D_p - T_{LS} - TT1_{j^{out}} - T_{QC} - TT3_{j^{ret}}$$

$K_i$ is the deadline slack — how much manufacturing time is available before the patient is late.

After observing $\tilde T_{MF,p}$, the standard (non-expedited) lateness would be:

$$L^{std}_p = \max(0,\; \tilde T_{MF,p} - K_i)$$

Expediting reduces TT3 by $\delta$ days (but costs $\pi_p$ extra):

$$L^{exp}_p = \max(0,\; \tilde T_{MF,p} - K_i - \delta)$$

Optimal Recourse Policy

Choose the option with lower total cost:

$$\text{recourse}(\tilde T_{MF,p}) = \min\!\left(\underbrace{w_p \cdot L^{std}_p}_{\text{no expedite}},\;\underbrace{\pi_p + w_p \cdot L^{exp}_p}_{\text{expedite}}\right)$$

Threshold Rule

Expanding the min, expediting is optimal when $\tilde T_{MF,p} > T^*_i$:

$$T^*_i = K_i + \frac{\pi_p}{w_p} \qquad \text{(expediting threshold for plan } i\text{)}$$

Zone 1: $\tilde T_{MF} \le K_i$

Patient is on time even without expediting.
Recourse cost = 0

Zone 2: $K_i < \tilde T_{MF} \le T^*_i$

Patient late, but expediting premium $\pi_p$ exceeds lateness saved.
Accept lateness: pay $w_p(\tilde T_{MF}-K_i)$

Zone 3: $\tilde T_{MF} > T^*_i$

Delay large enough that expediting saves more than it costs.
Expedite: pay $\pi_p + w_p\max(0,\tilde T_{MF}-K_i-\delta)$

Full Two-Stage Stochastic Formulation with Expediting Recourse

Uncertainty Model

$$T_{MF} \sim \text{LogNormal}(\mu_L,\, \sigma_L^2), \quad \mu_L = \ln(\bar T_{MF}) - \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma_L^2, \quad \mathbb{E}[T_{MF}] = \bar T_{MF}$$

Two-Stage Objective

$$\min_{x,z} \quad \underbrace{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} c_i\, x_i + \sum_{m \in M} f_m\, z_m}_{\text{Stage 1: transport + facility cost}} \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} x_i \cdot \mathbb{E}\left[R_i(T_{MF})\right]}_{\text{Stage 2: expected recourse cost}}$$

where the expected recourse cost for plan $i$ (patient $p$, slack $K_i$, threshold $T^*_i = K_i + \pi_p/w_p$) is:

$$\mathbb{E}[R_i(T_{MF})] = \underbrace{w_p \int_{K_i}^{T^*_i} (t - K_i)\, f(t)\, dt}_{\text{lateness in Zone 2}} + \underbrace{\int_{T^*_i}^{\infty} \!\left[\pi_p + w_p\max(0,\, t - K_i - \delta)\right] f(t)\, dt}_{\text{expediting cost in Zone 3}}$$

Constraints (identical to deterministic ColGen)

$$\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_p} x_i = 1 \quad \forall p \in P \tag{Assign}$$ $$\sum_{m \in M} z_m \le M_{\max} \tag{FacLimit}$$ $$x_i \le z_{m(i)} \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{I} \tag{FacLink}$$ $$\sum_{i:\, m(i)=m,\, t \in [s_i,\, s_i+T_{MF})} x_i \le FCAP_m \quad \forall m,t \tag{Cap}$$ $$x_i \in \{0,1\},\quad z_m \in \{0,1\} \tag{Binary}$$

Reformulation: Modified Column Cost

Because patients are assigned to exactly one plan, the expected recourse separates per column. Define the modified cost:

$$\tilde c_i = c_i + \mathbb{E}[R_i(T_{MF})] \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad \min \sum_i \tilde c_i\, x_i + \sum_m f_m\, z_m$$

The master IP structure is identical to deterministic ColGen. Only $c_i \to \tilde c_i$. No scenarios, no Benders cuts, no second-stage solver.

Closed-Form Expected Recourse Cost

For $T_{MF} \sim \text{LogNormal}(\mu_L, \sigma_L^2)$, let $\Phi$ be the standard normal CDF. Define:

$$K_i = D_p - T_{LS} - TT1 - T_{QC} - TT3 \quad\text{(slack)}, \qquad T^*_i = K_i + \frac{\pi_p}{w_p} \quad\text{(expediting threshold)}$$

Helper: Truncated LogNormal Expected Excess

Define $\rho(K, \bar T, \sigma_L) = \mathbb{E}[\max(0, T_{MF} - K)]$ (Black-Scholes call formula):

$$\rho(K) = \begin{cases} \bar T_{MF} - K & K \le 0 \\ \bar T_{MF}\,\Phi(d_1) - K\,\Phi(d_2) & K > 0 \end{cases}$$ $$d_1 = \frac{\mu_L + \sigma_L^2 - \ln K}{\sigma_L}, \qquad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma_L$$

Expected Recourse Cost (Three-Zone Decomposition)

$$\mathbb{E}[R_i(T_{MF})] = \underbrace{w_p\left[\rho(K_i) - \rho(T^*_i)\right]}_{\text{Zone 2 lateness}} + \underbrace{\pi_p\,\Pr(T_{MF} > T^*_i) + w_p\,\rho(T^*_i + \delta) - w_p\,\delta\,\Pr(T_{MF} > T^*_i+\delta)}_{\text{Zone 3 expediting}}$$

Where $\Pr(T_{MF} > K) = 1 - \Phi\!\left(\frac{\ln K - \mu_L}{\sigma_L}\right)$ is the LogNormal survival function.

Special Cases

Never Expedite ($\pi_p \ge w_p \cdot \delta$)

Premium exceeds maximum possible savings. $T^*_i \ge K_i + \delta$, Zone 3 reduces to penalty-only.
$$\mathbb{E}[R_i] = w_p \cdot \rho(K_i)$$ (same as penalty-only two-stage)

Always Expedite When Late ($\pi_p \to 0$)

Free expediting. $T^*_i \to K_i$, Zone 2 vanishes.
$$\mathbb{E}[R_i] = \pi_p \cdot \Pr(T_{MF}>K_i) + w_p\,\rho(K_i+\delta)$$

Step-by-Step Computation for Each Plan $i$

1
Compute slack $K_i = D_p - T_{LS} - TT1_{j^{out}} - T_{QC} - TT3_{j^{ret}}$
2
Compute threshold $T^*_i = K_i + \pi_p / w_p$
3
Compute $d_1, d_2$ at $K_i$, $T^*_i$, and $T^*_i+\delta$ using $\mu_L, \sigma_L$
4
Evaluate $\rho(K_i)$, $\rho(T^*_i)$, $\rho(T^*_i+\delta)$ and survival probabilities
5
Sum zones 2 and 3 to get $\mathbb{E}[R_i]$
6
Set $\tilde c_i = c_i + \mathbb{E}[R_i]$ and pass to ColGen master

Formulation Comparison

Deterministic ColGen

Obj: $\min\sum c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
Plans removed: infeasible only
Deadline: hard, $\bar T_{MF}=7$
Recourse: none
Tuning: none
Guarantee: none under uncertainty

CCP Stochastic ColGen

Obj: $\min\sum c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
Plans removed: low SL routes removed
Deadline: probabilistic $\Pr(TRT\le D)\ge 1-\alpha$
Recourse: none
Tuning: $\alpha$
Guarantee: $1-\alpha$ service level per patient

Two-Stage + Penalty Only

Obj: $\min\sum \tilde c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
$\tilde c_i = c_i + w_p \cdot \rho(K_i)$
Plans removed: none
Deadline: soft (penalised)
Recourse: accept lateness
Tuning: $w_p$
Guarantee: min expected cost

Two-Stage + Expediting Recourse (this formulation)

Objective: $\min\sum \tilde c_i x_i + \sum f_m z_m$
$\tilde c_i = c_i + \mathbb{E}[R_i(T_{MF})]$ (three-zone expected recourse)

Plans removed: none (all routes available)
Deadline: soft, but with active recourse
Recourse actions: expedite return (pay $\pi_p$, save $\delta$ days) or accept penalty $w_p$/day

Tuning: $w_p$ (penalty weights), $\pi_p$ (expediting premiums), $\delta$ (days saved by expediting)
Guarantee: minimises total expected cost including recourse
Scenarios needed: No — analytical closed form
Solver change: None — same ColGen master IP
