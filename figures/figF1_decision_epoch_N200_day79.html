Who each policy starts

same state, same two slots
FIFO p49r1p49r2 Earliest collection date first. Risk tier and survival are not inputs, so the two longest-waiting patients go — both low risk.
Survival index p44r3p18r0 αu · [ Su(t) − Su(t+1) ] — the life-value-weighted survival lost to one more day of waiting. Its curve is 23× steeper than the low-risk patients FIFO takes, so it wins having waited no days at all.
Adaptive MPC p44r3p18r0 A 7-day look-ahead MILP over the same survival curves. Same two patients identical choice

Survival decays exponentially, so the steepest one-day drop is at the top of the curve: within a tier both survival-aware policies serve the newest arrival, not the longest waiting. Across 1,843 contested epochs the index and the MPC never differ except on exact ties.
